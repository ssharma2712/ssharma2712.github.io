# About me

I am a computational biologist working at the intersection of evolutionary biology, statistics, and machine learning. My goal is to make inference from large genomic datasets more efficient and reliable. I am also interested in comparative genomics, evolutionary medicine, functional genomics, and genotype–phenotype relationships.

## Awards and professional activities

I received a Bangladesh Sweden Trust Fund travel grant (2018) and merit scholarships from the Institute of Statistical Research and Training, University of Dhaka (2013–2017). I am a member of the Society of Molecular Biology and Evolution and the Society for the Study of Evolution, and I review manuscripts for journals in evolutionary biology and bioinformatics.

01 / RESEARCH

Questions and methods

Efficient phylogenomics

Subsampling and upsampling strategies that reduce the computational demands of model selection and inference from large sequence alignments.

Confidence in evolutionary trees

Little bootstraps and related approaches for assessing support and uncertainty in genome-scale phylogenies.

Evolutionary sparse learning

Methods to identify the sequences that drive evolutionary relationships, reveal fragile clades, and study genotype–phenotype associations.

04 / BACKGROUND

From statistics to evolution

I earned a PhD in Biology at Temple University in 2023 after completing bachelor's and master's degrees in Applied Statistics at the University of Dhaka. My work brings these fields together to improve inference from large genomic datasets, with interests spanning comparative genomics, evolutionary medicine, and functional genomics.

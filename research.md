# Research

My work combines evolutionary biology, statistics, and computation to address questions that become difficult at genomic scale.

## Little bootstraps and support for phylogenies

I co-developed little bootstraps, which estimates confidence limits for large phylogenies using small subsets of alignment sites. My later work examines robust and efficient confidence limits for organismal relationships.

## Phylogenomic subsampling and upsampling

Subsampling methods can reduce the time and resources needed to analyze very large alignments. With ModelTamer and related work, I have studied how small samples of site patterns can support substitution model selection and other evolutionary analyses.

## Evolutionary sparse learning

I use sparse machine learning to identify sequences associated with phylogenetic hypotheses and to discover fragile clades. This work includes DrPhylo and contributions to MyESL, a software platform for evolutionary sparse learning.
